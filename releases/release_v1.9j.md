- **Thoughts spoken out loud.** When a long thought ran onto a second text page, that second page started without its opening bracket, so the game treated it as speech and the speaker's mouth moved while they were only thinking. Every thought now closes its bracket at the page break and opens a new one on the next page, the way Capcom's own script does it: "(Now that I'm standing in his shoes...)" and then "(...I'm starting to understand something else Naruhodo-san goes through.)". That covers every split thought in both games and in the first game's DLC. Thanks to GlacialSkyfarer for spotting it.
